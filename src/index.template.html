Events
The Invitation

This September 24–26, for the seventh anniversary of Robert Hunter’s passing, we’ll honor the lyricist and poet. Taking place across Portland, Oregon—the Kennedy School, Sanctuary Hall, Laurelthirst Public House, and Music Millennium will host readings, talks, and performances, bringing “Hunter heads” together.

Organized by poets Katherine Factor and Darrin Daniel, the event features writers Alan Trist, Jesse Jarnow, David Dodd, and Dr. Natalie Dollar, the Garcia Birthday Band, Lewi Longmire and Friends. Join us in exploring Hunter’s influences and profound impact on American song and poetry—while we muse on all that is yet unsung.

When one speaks the name “Robert Hunter,” it rings sweetly to those whose lives were shaped by his words. Grateful Dead lyricist. Poet, muse amplifier, maker of the new American Songbag.

Enigma to many · troubadour to all

Though he lived a life opaque to us, he nevertheless intimately alchemized the hearts of Deadheads—shaping their culture into a national treasure. His songs are responsible for millions of life milestones, business names, lovers and sordid heartbreaks. His words charged internal illuminations, a spectrum of Americana turned to personal ecstatics.

Featuring

The Guests

Jesse Jarnow

Author · Deadcast host

Jesse Jarnow is the author of Heads: A Biography of Psychedelic America (2016), Wasn’t That a Time (2018), and Big Day Coming (2012). He co-hosts the Good Ol’ Grateful Deadcast, the Grateful Dead’s official podcast, and has contributed liner notes for numerous Dead and Robert Hunter projects. His writing has appeared in Pitchfork, WIRED, Rolling Stone, the Village Voice, Dupree’s Diamond News, Relix, and elsewhere. Since 2008 he has hosted The Frow Show on freeform WFMU, and Da Capo will publish his next book, The Invisible Hit Parade: A Taper’s History of Music. More at JesseJarnow.com.

Alan Trist

Author · Ice Nine director

Alan Trist, an integral part of the Hunter-Garcia friendship, is the former director of the Grateful Dead’s music publisher, Ice Nine. He is the author of Water of Life: A Tale of the Grateful Dead. He appears in multiple Deadcast episodes, The Silver Snarling Trumpet, and Long Strange Trip (2017).

Dr. Natalie J. Dollar

Grateful Dead scholar · Professor

Natalie Dollar is Associate Professor of Speech Communication at Oregon State University, teaching across the Speech Communication, Social Science, Liberal Studies, and American Studies programs. She serves on the boards of Grateful Dead Studies and Phish Studies, and is the anthology editor of Concepts We’ll Ponder: Identity, Improvisation, and Community in the Phish Experience. Her work also appears in Reading the Grateful Dead (N. Meriwether, ed.) and The Grateful Dead in Concert: Essays on Live Improvisation (McFarland). She received the OSU-Cascades Teaching Award for 2011–2012.

David Dodd

Librarian · Author

David Dodd is the now-retired librarian author/editor of three books about the Grateful Dead, most notably The Complete Annotated Grateful Dead Lyrics (Simon and Schuster), which includes a foreword by Robert Hunter and an afterword by John Perry Barlow, as well as The Grateful Dead and the Deadheads: An Annotated Bibliography (Greenwood Press), and The Grateful Dead Reader (Oxford Univ. Press). Dodd authored the weekly blog “Greatest Stories Ever Told” about the songs for Dead.net and has contributed to various publications, including for the program notes for First Fusion—a symphonic collaboration by the Marin Symphony with Bob Weir, for which he also sang backup for Bobby! He also played in the Grateful Dead tribute band Dead Again.

About

Your Hosts

Katherine Factor

EDITOR · AUTHOR · POET

Katherine is an editor, book coach, and author of four Choose Your Own Adventures. Her debut poetry book, A Sybil Society, won the Interim Poetics Test Site Poetry Prize. A graduate of the Iowa Writers’ Workshop, she has appeared at the Oregon Country Fair, the Miami Book Festival, Mission Creek Music & Arts Festival, the Brautigan Library, Literary Arts, Skylight Books, Books Are Magic, and in libraries and schools across America. Her poems live online, in print, and on KBOO and WFMU.

Darrin Daniel

Publisher · Poetry editor

Darrin is the publisher of Cityful Press and poetry editor for Emergency Horse. A student of Allen Ginsberg, Harry Smith, and Robert Hunter at Naropa University, and raised in Eugene, Oregon, he has authored, edited, and published work on Harry Smith, Stan Brakhage, Gordon Ball’s Dark Music, and Hunter’s chapbook Infinity Minus Eleven. He interviewed Robert Hunter in 1993 and hosted a reading of his poems at the Boulder Bookstore in July 1994.
